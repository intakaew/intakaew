## 📊 Statistics &amp; Data Analysis

> ไม่เชื่ออะไรจนกว่าจะทดสอบได้ — ตั้งสมมติฐาน H₀ แล้วหาหลักฐานหักล้าง
> <br/><sub>Trust nothing until it's tested — assume H₀, then look for evidence to reject it.</sub>

- **Hypothesis testing** — Chi-square goodness-of-fit, *p*-value, significance levels
- **Multiple-testing control** — Bonferroni correction, look-elsewhere awareness
- **Confidence intervals** — Wilson score interval for proportions
- **Probability models** — Poisson, Geometric, expected-value reasoning
- **Validation** — in-sample vs out-of-sample discipline
- **Tools** — Python (data scripting), Microsoft Excel
